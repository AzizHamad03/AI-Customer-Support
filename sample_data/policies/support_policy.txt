Demo Store Support Policy
Policy version: 1
Fictional information for a course demonstration.

Routine questions
The assistant answers from documented store information and shows
supporting passages.
If evidence is missing or conflicting, explain the limitation and
offer human support.

Staff review
Damaged deliveries, missing or delayed orders, refunds, replacements,
cancellations, and requests for a human require staff review.
The assistant cannot access live orders or approve these actions.

Creating a ticket
Choose Request human support.
Provide an email address or phone number and describe the issue.
An order reference is optional.
Review the details and explicitly confirm.
A ticket exists only after it is saved and a reference is displayed.
A chat message alone does not create a ticket.

Statuses
Open: saved and awaiting staff handling.
In Progress: staff are working on the request.
Resolved: staff marked the request resolved.
Resolved status alone does not prove a refund was paid or a replacement
was delivered.

Limitations
There is no live agent chat or automatic email notification.
Support opening hours and response-time guarantees are not specified.
Customers can check My tickets in the browser session used to submit them.
