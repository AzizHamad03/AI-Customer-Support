Demo Store Shipping Policy
Policy version: 2
Fictional replacement for version 1 after successful indexing.

Delivery coverage
We ship to Riyadh and other cities within Saudi Arabia.
International shipping is not offered.

Updated shipping cost
Standard shipping costs SAR 20 when the merchandise subtotal after
discounts is below SAR 250.
Standard shipping is free when that subtotal is SAR 250 or more.
The shipping fee does not count toward the free-shipping threshold.
These rules replace the previous shipping prices and threshold.

Delivery estimates
Riyadh: 2-4 business days after dispatch.
Other Saudi cities: 3-7 business days after dispatch.
These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates.
Business days are Sunday through Thursday, excluding public holidays.

Delivery issues
Report damaged deliveries within 48 hours of receiving the package.
Staff must review damaged, delayed, or missing deliveries.
A report does not automatically approve a refund or replacement.

Tracking
The assistant cannot access live courier tracking or verify an order.
Customers may include an order reference in a support ticket.
